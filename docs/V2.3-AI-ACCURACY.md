# APEX Vision AI v2.3 — AI Accuracy

## Objective

v2.3 adds deterministic quality signals around the existing AI scene-analysis pipeline. The goal is to detect degraded floor geometry before a render is presented as trustworthy, without coupling quality scoring to a specific AI provider.

## Quality signals

`SceneQualityEvaluator` evaluates:

- Floor-mask coverage against sane bounds.
- Valid depth-pixel ratio.
- Floor polygon existence, finiteness and minimum area.
- Homography shape, finiteness and non-degenerate determinant.
- A 0–100 composite score and grade.

Grades:

- `excellent`: 90–100
- `good`: 75–89.99
- `review`: 60–74.99
- `poor`: below 60

Invalid scene inputs are explicitly reported as `invalid` rather than silently treated as successful analysis.

## Render integration

The quality report is attached to `SceneResult.metadata["quality"]` immediately before tile rendering and is included in the normal render-stage instrumentation. This makes the signal available for diagnostics and future API/UI exposure without changing the existing render contract.

## Regression gate

`tests/test_scene_quality.py` provides deterministic tests for valid geometry, missing geometry and invalid scenes. CI executes this suite as a dedicated AI-quality gate in addition to the full backend suite and performance baseline.

## Next accuracy phase

The deterministic gate is deliberately provider-independent. Real-world model accuracy improvements should be introduced using a versioned benchmark corpus of representative room images and reviewed ground-truth masks/polygons. That corpus should be added before claiming model-level accuracy percentages; synthetic unit fixtures alone are not evidence of real-world segmentation accuracy.
